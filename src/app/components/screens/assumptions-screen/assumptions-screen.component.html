Rental Properties (Schedule E)

Rental income is taxed differently from pensions or wages — gross rent minus operating expenses, mortgage interest, and depreciation flows to Schedule E. Depreciation is a paper expense that reduces taxable income while cash flow stays positive (residential straight-line over 27.5 years per IRS Pub 527). Year fields are sim-year offsets: 0 = retirement-start year. Negative depreciation start years mean the property was placed in service before the sim window begins.
