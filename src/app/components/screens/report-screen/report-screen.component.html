Narrative Report

Generates a Markdown-formatted walkthrough of your current plan and all selected locations. Designed to feed back into video / voiceover generation — not for spreadsheet export.

NEW

What's in the report

  • Household: members, ages, retirement horizon, target income.
  • Financial state: portfolio, allocation, expected return, savings rate.
  • FIRE math: FIRE number, progress, years remaining at current pace.
  • Cash flow: Social Security projection, portfolio withdrawal, expected taxes.
  • Guardrails bands: floor / base / ceiling withdrawal ranges.
  • Per-location chapters (one per selected location) — costs, climate, healthcare, visa, inclusion, neighborhoods, community & services, local info & resources.
  • Front-matter with machine-readable numbers so the narrative can be verified against structured data.
