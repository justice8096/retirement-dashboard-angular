Non-spouse beneficiaries of a traditional IRA from a decedent who died after 2019 must drain the account within 10 years. Distributions are taxed as ordinary income (not LTCG / QDI), so each drain year inflates MAGI by the per-year distribution. The kernel applies the post-tax distribution to portfolio balance and ripples the pre-tax distribution into the pre-65 ACA-subsidy calc.
